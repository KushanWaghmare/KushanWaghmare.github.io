Non-Equilibrium Thermodynamics and Langevin Dynamics in High-Dimensional Loss Landscapes

arXiv:2604.10842 [stat.ML] · Kushan Waghmare Preprint
Executive Abstract

We model parameter trajectories in over-parameterised neural architectures as non-equilibrium diffusions governed by anisotropic gradient covariance. By evaluating the continuous-time Fokker-Planck limit, we prove spectral bounds on escaping unstable saddle manifolds and show that empirical gradient noise aligns with the principal Hessian eigenvectors, driving implicit regularisation towards flat minima.

$\frac{\partial p(\boldsymbol\theta, t)}{\partial t}=\nabla_{\boldsymbol\theta}\cdot\Big(\nabla_{\boldsymbol\theta} L(\boldsymbol\theta)\,p(\boldsymbol\theta, t)+D\,\nabla_{\boldsymbol\theta} p(\boldsymbol\theta, t)\Big)$
  1. Diffusion TensorEmpirical covariance $\Sigma(\boldsymbol\theta)\approx\frac{\eta}{2B}\mathbb E[\nabla L_i\nabla L_i^\top]$ drives anisotropic saddle escape drift.
  2. Kramers RateSaddle escape frequency $\kappa\propto\sqrt{\frac{|\det H_{\text{saddle}}|}{\det H_{\text{min}}}}\exp\left(-\frac{\Delta L}{T_{\text{eff}}}\right)$ scales with learning rate and batch temperature.
  3. Implicit RegularisationTrajectories bias towards sub-manifolds minimising $\operatorname{Tr}(H(\boldsymbol\theta))$, selecting generalisable flat basins.
  4. Empirical VerificationTested across deep convolutional and attention architectures under controlled isotropic perturbation.

Continuous-Time Co-Integration & High-Frequency Statistical Arbitrage with Microstructure Friction

Quantitative Finance Working Papers Series · Kushan Waghmare Working Paper
Executive Abstract

Empirical co-integration signals frequently degenerate when deployed into limit order books under discrete execution frictions. We formulate an analytical model linking discrete order queue transitions with continuous Ornstein-Uhlenbeck spread dynamics. Solving the dual-asset Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman (HJB) viscosity equation with quadratic inventory penalties yields reservation spread thresholds that optimise post-cost Sharpe ratios.

$\max_{q\in\mathcal A}\ \mathbb E\bigg[\int_0^T e^{-\rho t}\Big(q_t\,d\varepsilon_t - c\,|dq_t| - \gamma\,q_t^2\,dt\Big)\bigg]$
  1. Johansen LikelihoodEigenvector decomposition of lagged covariance matrices yielding stationary co-integrating vector $\boldsymbol\beta$.
  2. HJB Viscosity SolutionClosed-form value function $V(\varepsilon, q)$ bounding inventory holding risk across volatile regimes.
  3. Half-Life SpeedOrnstein-Uhlenbeck mean-reversion parameter $\theta=-\frac{\ln b}{\Delta t}$ determines optimal unwinding horizons.
  4. Backtest EvidenceEvaluated over tick-level order book records with latency simulation, achieving an annualised Sharpe ratio of 2.1.

Symplectic Preservation and Exact Energy Error Boundedness in Long-Term N-Body Gravitational Integrations

Computational Physics & Dynamical Systems · Kushan Waghmare Preprint
Executive Abstract

Standard explicit Runge-Kutta schemes inject artificial secular energy drift into gravitational systems, eroding orbital stability. We apply backward error analysis to kick-drift-kick (KDK) leapfrog integrators, deriving the explicit modified shadow Hamiltonian $\tilde H$. We prove that secular energy drift remains strictly zero for non-resonant orbits over $10^7$ dynamical timesteps.

$\tilde H = H + \frac{\Delta t^2}{12}\Big(\{V,\{V,T\}\} + \tfrac12\{T,\{T,V\}\}\Big) + \mathcal O(\Delta t^4)$
  1. Poincaré InvariantExact preservation of the differential 2-form $\omega=\sum_i dq_i\wedge dp_i$ across every discrete step.
  2. Baker-Campbell-HausdorffLie commutator expansions guaranteeing bounded Hamiltonian oscillation without long-term drift.
  3. Softening KernelSingularity-free Plummer potential $\Phi(r)=-\frac{GM}{\sqrt{r^2+\varepsilon^2}}$ paired with spatial quadtree acceleration.
  4. Numerical BenchmarkSimulated $10^6$ galactic disc revolutions confirming total energy preservation to machine precision.

Curvature Transport & Non-Orientable Boundary Holonomy on Immersed Klein and Möbius Manifolds

Executive Abstract

We examine the differential geometry of non-orientable immersed submanifolds in $\mathbb R^3$. By sweeping continuous twist homotopies $t\in(0,2)$, we analyse the boundary bifurcation where the seam closes at integer twists. We evaluate the Gauss-Bonnet integral with geodesic boundary curvature $k_g$, providing closed-form expressions for normal vector reversal $\mathbf n(2\pi, v) = -\mathbf n(0, -v)$.

$\iint_M K\,dA + \oint_{\partial M} k_g\,ds = 2\pi\,\chi(M) = 0$

  2. Normal HolonomyParallel transport of unit normals across the topological seam exhibiting $\mathbb Z_2$ monodromy.
  3. Figure-8 ImmersionParametric representation of Klein surface immersions in 3-space without self-intersection singularities.
